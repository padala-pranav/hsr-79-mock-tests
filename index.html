Segmented tab pills for Full Tests, Chapter-wise, and Subject-wise. 2. Mock Test cards display: Title, Subtitle, 3 Hours duration, 200 Questions, Difficulty badge (Moderate/Difficult). 3. Motivational "Track Your Progress" banner. --- ### ISSUE 2: [SUSHRUTA-102] NTA-Style CBT Live Exam Simulator Engine - **Type:** Story | **Priority:** Urgent | **Estimate:** 5 pts - **Summary:** Real-time exam test engine adhering to official NTA CBT interface. - **Acceptance Criteria:** 1. 200-minute live countdown timer with warning alert (<5 mins) and auto-submit. 2. Subject Section navigation (Physics Sec A/B, Chemistry Sec A/B, Botany Sec A/B, Zoology Sec A/B). 3. Full Question Palette with NTA state indicators (Answered, Not Answered, Marked for Review, Answered & Marked, Not Visited). 4. Actions: "Clear Response", "Mark for Review & Next", "Save & Next", "Submit Test". --- ### ISSUE 3: [SUSHRUTA-103] Instant Scorecard, AIR Predictor & Analytics Engine - **Type:** Story | **Priority:** High | **Estimate:** 5 pts - **Summary:** Immediate scoring engine applying NEET UG marking (+4, -1, 0) and predictive rank analysis. - **Acceptance Criteria:** 1. Score calculated out of 720 with percentile and AIR prediction range. 2. Medical College Cut-off Barometer (AIIMS, State GMCs, BDS, Private). 3. Subject-wise accuracy and score breakdown across Physics, Chemistry, Botany, and Zoology. --- ### ISSUE 4: [SUSHRUTA-104] Interactive Mistake Book, NCERT Solutions & Re-Attempt Flow - **Type:** Story | **Priority:** Medium | **Estimate:** 3 pts - **Summary:** In-depth solution walkthrough and one-click test re-attempt to measure score improvement. - **Acceptance Criteria:** 1. Filter solutions by All, Incorrect, Skipped, and Correct. 2. Step-by-step explanations with scientific formula rendering and NCERT page citations. 3. "Re-attempt Test" button resets state and records longitudinal score progress.
